# GNS preview research pack
Collected 28 October 2026 for Zayvo AI Studio.

## Included
- 37 downloaded website image assets (logo/favicon, property imagery, team thumbnails and developer logos).
- asset_sources.json: exact original URL and download size for every asset; failures if any.
- ASSET_GALLERY.html: offline contact sheet; open in a browser.
- business_profile.json: public business contacts and source URLs.
- SOCIAL_PROFILES.md: official linked accounts and access limitations.
- property_links.csv: property titles and exact source pages from the homepage.
- PREVIEW_BRIEF.md: proposed enquiry-assistant demo flow.

This is a research and asset pack for making the preview, not a deployed chatbot or complete social-media archive. Social feeds were inaccessible. Website imagery may include developer renders, not photographs of completed buildings. Images retain original filenames. Availability, prices, office and team details require client confirmation before publication.
