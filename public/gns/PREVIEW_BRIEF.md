# GNS enquiry-assistant preview brief
Prepared for Hadi / Zayvo AI Studio

## Goal
Demonstrate the enquiry assistant offered in the outreach: collect buyer requirements and prepare an agent handoff. GNS already has property listings, enquiry forms and WhatsApp links. The demo should enhance that workflow.

## Suggested preview
1. Branded welcome with original GNS logo and a property image.
2. Ask Buy / Rent / Off-plan investment.
3. Ask preferred community, budget range, property type and bedrooms.
4. Ask purchase/move timeline and preferred contact method.
5. Show a concise requirement summary and an example agent handoff.
6. Explain that an agent confirms availability, pricing and viewing appointments.

## Demo scenarios
- Buyer: 2-bedroom apartment, Dubai Creek Harbour, AED 2–3m, within 3 months.
- Rental: apartment, Business Bay, annual budget AED 100–150k.
- Investor: off-plan, wants payment-plan information; escalate specifics to agent.
These are invented demonstration inputs, not real customers.

## Content and design
Use downloaded logo as brand reference; do not invent approved colours or fonts. Use property images as reference imagery, maintaining their source attribution. Some filenames and displayed project names differ: do not map images to projects solely by filename. Property links are supplied for verification. Team headshots may be thumbnails; do not upscale them as if high-resolution originals.

## Client confirmation needed
Current office address; authorised agent roster and routing; approved live inventory and prices; available languages; CRM integration; reservation/calendar workflow; approved asset usage. Public images are not a licence to republish developer marketing.

## Avoid
No guaranteed ROI, invented reviews, fake availability, unsupported sales statistics or real outgoing messages in the demo. Website sales-volume claims are self-published and unverified. Do not describe GNS as losing business.
